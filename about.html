ABOUT

Welcome to the DTI Educational Resource Hub, a digital platform designed to support the teaching and learning of Digital Technology and Innovation (DTI) in Bhutanese schools.

About DTI Curriculum

What is DTI?

DTI stands for Digital Technology and Innovation. Innoloom supports Class IX learners with self-paced, practical and curriculum-aligned learning resources.

Purpose of the Curriculum

The curriculum develops digital competencies together with creativity, critical thinking, communication and collaboration.

Key Stages and Coverage

This course covers Internet and Web Technologies, Web Server, Web Browser, Website, HTML and the System Development Life Cycle.

How this site maps to the curriculum

Each section combines explanation, practice, interactive media and assessment so learners can move from knowledge to application.
